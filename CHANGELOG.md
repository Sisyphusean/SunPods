# Changelog

## 2.29.0

A new look, and nearly everything Sony's app can do for the WF-1000XM5 (not yet: Quick Access, voice guidance language and firmware updates).

**Redesign**
- Shorter tabs: each setting is one row with its value, and the details live one tap deeper on their own page
- Material You icons on tonal shapes that follow your wallpaper colours
- A rebuilt Battery tab: listening time left, a clearer history chart you can scrub, and a week view you can tap for each day
- An animated tab bar, Material page transitions and predictive back (swipe back to peek at where you're going)
- Sheets, menus and expanding sections open and close smoothly; picking an option shows your choice before the sheet slides away
- Readings show instantly. Only a new reading animates, so you can see what changed
- Haptics for switches and choices, a turning refresh button, and a slim bar while a change is saving
- Clearer, friendlier wording throughout

**New earbud features**
- Now Playing with playback controls and volume
- Head gestures: nod to answer, shake to decline
- Voice assistant choice
- Ear tip fit test
- Turn off earbuds, and reset earbud settings
- Switch devices automatically, Safe Listening and Volume Protection
- A While Running automation, and Auto Play when you take your earbuds out

## 2.28.0

The first public release.

- Switch between Classic and LE Audio from the app, with Shizuku doing the pairing
- SunPods fixes a stuck connection by itself, and finishes the fix when Shizuku comes back
- On LE Audio, settings reach both earbuds, and features that need Classic are greyed out
- Touch options come from the earbuds' own list, with each gesture spelled out
- SunPods checks that your phone supports LE Audio before offering it
- A daily check for new versions on GitHub, which you can turn off
