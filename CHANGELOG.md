# Changelog

## 3.0.0

A new SunPods: redesigned from top to bottom, your earbuds in 3D and in their own colour, nearly everything Sony's app can do (not yet: voice guidance language and firmware updates), and it can update itself.

**A new look**
- Your earbuds in live 3D on Home, in their real colour (Black, Platinum Silver or Smoky Pink), read from the earbuds themselves. Drag to spin them. The pictures and setup animations across the app match too.
- Shorter tabs: each setting is one row with its value, and the details are one tap deeper
- Pages slide in like the rest of your phone, and swiping back drags the page off under your finger
- A floating tab bar that tucks away as you scroll, and each tab keeps its place
- A rebuilt Battery tab, and a case reading that greys out when it's old (the case only reports when you take the earbuds out)
- Clearer, friendlier wording throughout, and a redesigned setup

**New**
- A live listening level in decibels on the Safe Listening page
- Now Playing with playback controls and volume, head gestures, voice assistant choice, ear tip fit test, turn off and reset earbuds
- Switch devices automatically, Safe Listening and Volume Protection, a While Running automation, and Auto Play
- SunPods updates itself: a card on Home when there's a new version, one tap to install, and What's New after every update

**Fixes**
- Switching between Classic and LE Audio no longer asks for the case button when the phone already knows the earbuds, and one press now pairs both sides
- SunPods never removes a working pairing on its own any more
- Phones that list the earbuds more than once (common with LE Audio) now pick the right one
- Settings no longer snap back when the earbuds are slow to answer, and voice prompts can be switched on and off again
- Back from a page goes back to the page before, not out of the app
- Less battery: background checks stop when you're not looking and ease off when the earbuds don't answer

## 2.28.0

The first public release.

- Switch between Classic and LE Audio from the app, with Shizuku doing the pairing
- SunPods fixes a stuck connection by itself, and finishes the fix when Shizuku comes back
- On LE Audio, settings reach both earbuds, and features that need Classic are greyed out
- Touch options come from the earbuds' own list, with each gesture spelled out
- SunPods checks that your phone supports LE Audio before offering it
- A daily check for new versions on GitHub, which you can turn off
